## 1. The Service

Ustam is an intermediary app that lets users view nearby pros, create a request by choosing a problem type
and visit time, and receive a job code for every request. Ustam is not a party to the service provided by
the pros.

- Enter the problem and address note accurately and completely when creating a request.

- Do not use the app unlawfully or in a way that disturbs others.
