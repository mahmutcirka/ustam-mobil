# Privacy Policy

<p class="guncelleme">Last updated: 7 October 2026</p>

This app is an educational project developed for the İstinye University MYO063 Mobile Programming course.
Your privacy matters to us; this page explains which data is kept and where.

## 1. Data Collected and Local Storage

Ustam **does not send or store** your personal data on any external server. All preferences are kept only
in your device's **localStorage**:

- `profil` — your name, phone, address and door / floor note (only if you enter them)
- `ev-hazirligi` — your ticks in the "I know my home" list (water valve, fuse box, gas valve)
- `cagri-taslagi` and `is-emirleri` — your requests and job codes
- `dil` and `tema` — your language and light/dark mode preference

The contact form does not actually send anything in this educational version; what you type is not stored.

## 2. Data Security

Job codes are generated by the Rust core on your device (`is_emri_uret`) and are never sent over the
network. Running inside the Tauri WebView, the app uses no third-party tracking cookies, ads or analytics.

## 3. Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

Under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the **Protection of Personal Data (KVKK)**: the data you enter is processed
only to create requests and only on your own device; it is not shared with third parties. Under Article 11
of the KVKK you have the right to access your data and to request its correction or deletion. You can
change your data at any time on the **Profile** page, and delete it completely by clearing the app data or
uninstalling the app.

## 4. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the [Contact](/en/iletisim) page.
