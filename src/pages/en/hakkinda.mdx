# About Ustam

*Your breakdown, our pro.*

## Why Ustam?

A pipe bursting at midnight, a tripping fuse, or being locked out… In moments like these it is hard to
reach a trustworthy pro who is **available right now**. Ustam lists nearby pros with their rating, distance
and availability, lets you describe the problem in a few taps and call a pro immediately.

The app works in **Turkish, English, Arabic and Persian**. You pick your problem in your own language; the
pro receives the same request in Turkish. Thanks to the **job code** generated for every request, you can
verify that the person at your door is the pro you actually called.
